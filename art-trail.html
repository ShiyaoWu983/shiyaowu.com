Brand Concept

Art Trail is an art intelligence platform that reimagines how people discover, plan and engage with exhibitions. Built for art lovers, students and professionals, it offers curated journeys, personalised recommendations and one-stop planning — from local galleries to citywide explorations.

Research & Strategy

71% of Gen Z and Millennials say cultural experiences are their top reason to travel, prioritising museums and galleries over traditional sightseeing.

Yet art discovery is overwhelming: information is scattered, the experience feels impersonal, and visitors struggle to connect with exhibitions.

App & Website

Art Trail guides users through every step of their journey. They discover exhibitions through AI-powered recommendations and curated trails, plan visits with seamless ticketing, personalised itineraries and interactive maps, and engage beyond the gallery walls through reviews, reflections and community.

Kiosk

Developed with museums and galleries, the Art Trail kiosk is a physical touchpoint that connects visitors to the platform — exploring curated journeys, accessing exhibition information and sharing reviews on site. It bridges digital and physical experiences to deepen engagement with art.

Ad Campaign

Billboards and city-wide ads invite people to “Follow the Trail,” using the orange line-drawn skyline to turn the city itself into an art map.

Merchandise

Hoodies, socks and tote bags carry the trail line and skyline illustrations, letting the brand travel with its community.

AR Notebook

A notebook for taking notes during exhibition visits. Scanning a page lets AI summarise the visit, learn what each visitor responds to, and save it to their profile to shape future recommendations.

Stationery

Letterhead, envelopes and business cards extend the orange trail line and skyline across every printed touchpoint.
