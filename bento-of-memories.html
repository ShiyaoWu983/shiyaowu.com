Overview

Bento of Memories is a set of four handmade books, each built around an ingredient and a year of my life. Together they pair personal stories with recipes — from farewells in 2020 to a family reunion in 2023.

Tomato

Tomato reflects on 2020 — another year abroad, full of farewells and longing for family. The book’s deckled edges recall the skin of a real tomato, and its pages pair memories with the recipes that brought me closer to home.

Maitake

Maitake recalls a summer 2021 omakase dinner where I was the only guest. A plate of tempura maitake opened a story about Japan’s quiet forests, and the book is designed to carry readers into the place where these mushrooms grow.

Truffle

Truffle marks my 22nd birthday dinner in 2022. The semi-handmade book mimics the texture of a truffle and includes the recipe for the evening’s centrepiece dish, so readers experience it by touch as well as sight.

Salmon

Salmon is a scroll-shaped book about reunion, capturing 2023, when I saw my family again after four years apart. Short stories unfold alongside a recipe for a salmon poke bowl.
