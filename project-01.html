Brand Concept

Many first-time movers struggle to carry household supplies, remember which cleaning products they need, and understand how to look after a new home.

IKEA helps people furnish a home; FÖRSTA helps them learn how to live in it.

Product System

Six essentials in one kit: liquid dish soap, foam hand soap, multi-surface cleaner, 3-in-1 laundry pods, refill tablets and a microfiber cleaning cloth.

Brand Identity

The logo borrows the visual language of the IKEA wordmark, and the name comes from Swedish — första means “first.” A small house sits within the letterforms, setting a friendly, practical tone.

The colour palette is drawn directly from IKEA’s products, keeping the brand warm and approachable for its audience of college students and young professionals.

Product Lineup

Every product comes with its own refill tablet, so bottles are reused rather than replaced.

The familiar figure from IKEA’s instruction manuals appears on each product, customised into a different pose that shows how it is used.

Point-of-Purchase Display

The display simulates how the shelf would appear in a real IKEA store, presenting the full kit and its refills just as shoppers would encounter them.

Ad Campaign

A series of six posters follows the first days in a new home, pairing each product with a moment: “First Dinner. Then Dishes.” “First Load? Easy.” “Keep the Bottle. Refill the Rest.”

Assembly Instructions

Drawn in the style of IKEA’s instruction manuals, the booklet shows what comes in the kit, how to fit each pump and nozzle, and where to use each product around the home.

Website

FÖRSTA launches on IKEA’s website as a new collection, with a dedicated page where visitors can buy the Home Essentials Kit and its refill tablets — start with the kit, refill what you use.
